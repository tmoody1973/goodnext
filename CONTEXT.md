# GoodNext

A free website that helps a Wisconsin household find food this week and prepare an official next step after a FoodShare notice or life change. Food help never requires an account, a notice, or a benefits question.

## Language

**Food today**:
The set of options a resident can actually use on the current Milwaukee calendar date, shown first and expanded. The first shipped feature.
_Avoid_: Today's plan, day one, emergency food

**Bridge Plan**:
The anonymous seven-day food access plan, one dated entry per local calendar day starting with Food today. Feature 2; produced now but not yet promised.
_Avoid_: Seven-day plan, weekly plan, meal plan

**Opening window**:
A dated span of clock time during which a provider says it serves food. The only basis for calling an option "open today".
_Avoid_: Hours, schedule, slot

**Next open**:
The earliest opening window after the current moment for a resource that is not open now.
_Avoid_: Reopens, next available

**Service area**:
The set of ZIP codes a provider says it serves. Unknown when the record has none; unknown never means excluded.
_Avoid_: Coverage, catchment, radius

**Verified**:
A record whose details a named reviewer checked within the last 14 days. The only records shown without a caveat.
_Avoid_: Confirmed, current, fresh

**Call to confirm**:
The caveat on a record last verified 15 to 60 days ago. Shown with the check date and phone number.
_Avoid_: Stale, outdated, unverified

**Unconfirmed**:
A record last verified more than 60 days ago or never. Listed with a phone number only, never as a Food today visit.
_Avoid_: Expired, dead, closed

**Directions link**:
A link that opens the provider's address in the resident's own map app. The site's only travel guidance.
_Avoid_: Route, travel time, distance

**Listed option**:
A food resource the directory returned for the resident's area and date that passes the display rules. A listing, never a promise.
_Avoid_: Result, match, available option

**Planned visit**:
A listed option the resident marked as one they intend to try. An intention, not food.
_Avoid_: Booked, reserved, selected, secured

**Confirmed food**:
Food the resident reports actually receiving after a visit. The only thing that counts toward food covered.
_Avoid_: Obtained, secured, fulfilled

**Food covered**:
The portion of a day's need met by Confirmed food. Never inferred from listed options or planned visits.
_Avoid_: Food secured, sorted, handled

**Help route**:
A maintained, verified way to reach a human: 2-1-1, the Hunger Task Force emergency food page, or the FoodShare member line. Shown whenever the site cannot help.
_Avoid_: Fallback, referral, escalation

**No-match**:
The outcome when no listed option exists for the resident's ZIP and date. Always paired with help routes and the option to change ZIP.
_Avoid_: Empty state, no results, error
