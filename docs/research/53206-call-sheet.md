# 53206 call sheet: verify nine food sites by phone

For Tarik. About an hour. Each call takes five minutes. Write answers in the
blanks; I load them into the directory as **verified** records with you as
the verifier and the call date. Name, address, and phone below are public
facts. Hours and rules must come from the call, not from any map.

## What to say

> Hi, my name is Tarik. I'm building a free website that helps Milwaukee
> families find food. I'm confirming a few details so we don't send anyone
> to a closed door. Do you have two minutes?

## The seven questions

1. Are you giving out food this week? Which days and what hours?
2. Is it a pantry (take home) or a meal (eat there), or both?
3. Do people need to bring anything: ID, proof of address, a referral?
4. Do you serve people from any ZIP, or only certain areas?
5. Is there a limit, like once a month?
6. Is an appointment or a call ahead needed?
7. Is it okay if we list you on the site with these hours and this number?

Then: "Thank you. Who am I speaking with?" Write the first name and role.

## If nobody answers

Try once more on a different day. If still nothing, mark **no answer** and
leave the record out. An unreached site is not a listed option.
