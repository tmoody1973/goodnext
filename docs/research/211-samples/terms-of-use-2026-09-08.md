# 211 National Data Platform API portal: Terms of Use excerpt

Copied by Tarik from the Trial - V2 subscription page on September 8, 2026.
Kept as the compliance record for decision 005. Verbatim.

> **211 Data Ownership and Stewardship:** All 211 data exposed through this
> API is owned by the local 211 centers that contribute their data to the
> National Data Platform. United Way Worldwide serves as the steward of this
> data on behalf of participating 211 organizations.
>
> Any use of this data for production, operational, commercial, or other
> external purposes requires the prior permission of each participating 211
> center whose data is being accessed or used, in addition to compliance with
> any applicable agreements, policies, and legal requirements.

Usage limits (same for trial): 1,000 requests per rolling 24 hours per key;
10 requests per 60 seconds; anonymous requests share one pool; the API may
force default pagination and exclude inactive items; usage is monitored and
subscriptions may be suspended for misuse. Limits may change without notice.

Not present in these terms: any Creative Commons or non-commercial license
language. The CC BY-NC-SA claim seen in search results is refuted for the
API terms; it may describe something else.
